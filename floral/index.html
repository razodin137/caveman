What we make

Every piece starts with a conversation and ends with flowers cut at their peak.

Weddings

Bouquets, ceremony arches, aisle pieces, and reception tablescapes — built from the week’s best cut. We take a small number of weddings each season.

Peak booking · May–June, September–October

Weekly arrangements

A standing vase for homes and front desks across Lexington. We deliver fresh stems, carry away last week’s, and leave the vase looking at home.

Weekly or fortnightly · pause any week

Events & galas

Garlands long enough to cross a barn, centerpieces short enough to see over. Derby-week dinners, openings, and quiet goodbyes, all starting with a room walk-through.

Garlands · centerpieces · sympathy work

Rooted in the Bluegrass

We buy first from Kentucky flower farms — growers in Fayette, Scott, and Woodford counties who cut the morning we collect. What the farms can’t cover comes from our own cutting garden, and in deep winter from a short list of trusted growers farther south.

Limestone soil makes sweet stems in this part of the country. We think you can taste it in the color.

  • Kentucky-grown first, always
  • Season-driven, never forced
  • Compost what we don’t use
